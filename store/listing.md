## Full description (4000 chars max)

Plateato is the shared kitchen brain for your household. Track what's in your fridge, freezer, and pantry, see what's about to expire, and keep one shopping list everyone can update.

KNOW WHAT YOU HAVE
• Add items in seconds from a built-in catalog with smart defaults for units, storage location, and shelf life
• Organized by where things actually live: fridge, freezer, pantry, spice rack
• Quantities that match how you track food — exact amounts, package counts, or simple full/half/low

WASTE LESS
• Color-coded expiration indicators show what's fine, what's soon, and what's past
• The home dashboard surfaces what's expiring before it goes bad

SHOP TOGETHER
• One shared shopping list for the whole household
• Items grouped by aisle, checked off as you go
• Everyone sees the same list — no more duplicate milk

BUILT FOR HOUSEHOLDS
• Create a household and invite family or roommates with a simple code
• Changes stay in sync for everyone

Plateato is free and has no ads. Recipes that connect to your pantry are on the way.
